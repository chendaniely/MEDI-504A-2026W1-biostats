Exercise 1: Tumour growth over time

This exercise follows the tidy data chapter of Data Science for the Biomedical Sciences. Mice with a human glioma xenograft were assigned to one of 4 groups:

  • control (1.CTR)
  • drug (2.D)
  • radiation (3.R)
  • drug and radiation (4.D+R)

Tumour size (mm³) was measured over 28 days.

data/tumorgrowth_wide.csv is a version of the tumorgrowth data from the medicaldata GitHub repository. It’s ds4biomed’s data/tumorgrowth_long.csv (chendaniely/ds4biomed, learner branch), which we renamed because it’s in wide format.

Exercise 3: Cytomegalovirus

cytomegalovirus (from medicaldata) follows 64 patients after a stem cell transplant (Sobecks et al., 2011). Here are the columns we’ll recode.

  • cmv: whether CMV reactivated (0 = no, 1 = yes)
  • recipient.cmv: the patient’s CMV serostatus (0 = negative, 1 = positive)
  • donor.cmv: the donor’s CMV serostatus (0 = negative, 1 = positive)
NoteYour turn
  1. Make a data frame called cmv. Recode the cmv column as a factor with labels no and yes, and recipient.cmv and donor.cmv as factors with labels negative and positive.
  2. How many values are missing in time.to.transplant?
cmv <- cytomegalovirus |>
